## Safeguards and results

- Exclusions require the reviewed profile ID, expected source name and department,
  exact rejected local name, exact retained local name, and an already-attached
  retained-owner course. Missing or changed evidence leaves the case held.
- Stale or undated roster observations cannot revoke a newer stored association.
- The source transformer supplies department evidence only when present. The
  extension continues to use an offline snapshot.
- All previous display names, aliases, grade values, and 30,146 terms must survive
  after resolving retired IDs; repeated imports must be byte-identical.
- Other RMP profile IDs are neither pooled nor merged by these name rules.
- The former shared-profile queue is cleared. Remaining reviews concern competing
  source-name profiles and conflicting source IDs.

No merge, push, or publication is part of this local preview change.
