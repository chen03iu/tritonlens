## RateMyProfessors

Suggested recipient: support@ratemyprofessors.com

Subject: Noncommercial data-use permission request for TritonLens

Hello RateMyProfessors team,

I am building TritonLens, a free, noncommercial, privacy-focused Chrome
extension for UC San Diego students. It enhances UCSD's course-selection page
by showing concise instructor context beside instructor names.

I would like written permission to use a periodically generated local snapshot
of UCSD professor-level RateMyProfessors data, limited to overall rating,
difficulty, rating count, would-take-again percentage when available,
professor identifier, source URL, and last-updated date.

The extension would not collect student data, automate rating submissions, or
make live requests to RateMyProfessors when a student views a course. It would
clearly attribute RateMyProfessors, link to the relevant source page, identify
the snapshot date, and keep professor-level ratings distinct from historical
course-grade information.

Would RateMyProfessors permit TritonLens to retrieve, cache, transform, bundle,
and redistribute those fields in a free noncommercial browser extension? If
so, please specify any access method, refresh limits, attribution language,
removal process, or other conditions you require.

Thank you for considering the request.

## UCSD Associated Students

Suggested recipient: associatedstudents@ucsd.edu

Subject: Permission request for aggregate Instructor Grade Archive data

Hello Associated Students team,

I am building TritonLens, a free, noncommercial, privacy-focused Chrome
extension that helps UC San Diego students evaluate courses directly inside
the Triton Student System.

I would like permission to use aggregate records from the A.S. Instructor
Grade Archive. The proposed fields are academic term, course subject and
number, instructor name, aggregate grade counts or percentages, average GPA
when available, sample size, source URL, and snapshot date.

TritonLens would not collect or display individual student records, personal
grades, enrollment histories, or other protected student information.
Historical results would be labeled as historical context rather than a
prediction of future grades. The extension would provide attribution and a
link to the original A.S. source.

Would A.S. permit TritonLens to retrieve, transform, cache, bundle, and
redistribute these aggregate records in a free noncommercial browser
extension? If another UC San Diego office owns the data or must approve this
use, could you please direct me to the appropriate contact?

Please also let me know whether you require a particular access method,
refresh frequency, attribution statement, or deletion process.

Thank you.
